# Social Media Management and Listening

The rapid evolution of AI is fundamentally reshaping the social media management and listening market. This Magic Quadrant evaluates seven vendors and helps organizations select the right partner to seamlessly connect real-time social intelligence with comprehensive management execution.

## Market Definition

Gartner defines the social media management and listening market as SaaS platforms that enable organizations to comprehensively manage, monitor, listen, analyze, and optimize their social media presence and activities. These platforms support digital marketing leaders and other business functions in orchestrating, publishing, distributing, and engaging with content across social media platforms, while also enabling the collection, measurement, and interpretation of social interactions, conversations, and trends relevant to brands, industries, and target audiences.

Social media management and listening platforms empower organizations to strategically manage, analyze, and optimize their social presence across diverse channels. These solutions address critical business challenges such as fragmented brand messaging, delayed crisis response, inefficient customer engagement, and limited visibility into market sentiment. By centralizing social publishing, listening, engagement, and analytics, these platforms enable cross-functional teams — including marketing, communications, customer service, HR, and product development — to collaborate seamlessly and respond proactively to market dynamics.
