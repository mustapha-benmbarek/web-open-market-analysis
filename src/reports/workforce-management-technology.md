# Workforce Management (WFM) Technology

HR leaders seek WFM applications to enhance operational efficiency, ensure compliance and transform frontline workers’ experience. Use this Magic Quadrant to gain insights into leading WFM vendors and identify those most suitable to support your frontline workforce deployment.

## Market Definition

Gartner defines workforce management (WFM) technology as a solution comprising software, services and (often) hardware that helps organizations manage their workers’ operational deployment. HR technology and operations leaders seek WFM technology to enhance operational efficiency and transform frontline work.

WFM delivers capabilities to manage and optimize the operational deployment of the frontline workforce. This includes aligning staffing with demand, controlling labor costs, ensuring compliance, improving operational decision making and boosting frontline worker engagement and experience.
