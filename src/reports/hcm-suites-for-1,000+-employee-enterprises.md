# HCM Suites for 1,000+ Employee Enterprises

HCM suites are evolving into agentic systems that orchestrate outcomes across the workforce. CHROs and their HR technology leaders can use this Magic Quadrant research to evaluate vendors’ ability to deliver business value through an increasingly dynamic HCM technology landscape and AI-enabled capabilities.

## Market Definition

Gartner defines HCM suites for 1,000+ employee enterprises as cloud applications that deliver functionality for attracting, developing, engaging, administering and rewarding employees.
