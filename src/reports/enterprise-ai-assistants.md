# Enterprise AI Assistants

Enterprise AI assistants, such as ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude Enterprise, Gemini Enterprise and Microsoft Copilot, are rapidly becoming the new “front door” for work, as employees use agentic interfaces to research, analyze data, create content, and delegate tasks and actions. This Magic Quadrant helps application leaders evaluate their options based on key criteria.

## Market Definition

Gartner defines an enterprise AI assistant (EAIA) as an AI-first application, powered by one or more GenAI models. EAIAs are designed to augment human capabilities and support human-led actions by offering agentic RAG search, agentic tools and enterprise-grade security. The EAIA is becoming an essential “front door” application for employees to routinely use GenAI to streamline content creation, research, analysis and collaboration, and to access AI agents.

An EAIA is built to meet broad employee needs for services that help workers improve productivity, deliver higher-quality work and save time. These general-purpose tools differ from more narrowly focused or that are specific to a business function (e.g., marketing or customer service) or industry vertical (e.g., healthcare or legal), or are embedded in an enterprise application (e.g., contact center or HR applications). An EAIA should enable new ways of working by acting as more of a personal collaborator than a generic chatbot tool.
