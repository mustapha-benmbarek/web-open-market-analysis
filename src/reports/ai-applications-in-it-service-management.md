# AI Applications in IT Service Management

AI applications in IT service management use AI to augment and extend ITSM workflows, enabling organizations to deliver IT support to business users and intelligent advisory and actions for IT agents. This Magic Quadrant evaluates nine highly relevant vendors offering AI-focused ITSM products for heads of I&O.

## Market Definition

Gartner defines AI applications in IT service management as tools that augment and enhance IT service management (ITSM) workflows using AI. These tools analyze ITSM data and metadata (primarily found in ITSM platforms) to provide intelligent advice and execute actions on ITSM practices and workflows, such as IT service desk and support activities. The software can be delivered as an incumbent ITSM platform with integrated AI features, a native AI add-on or a third-party product that integrates with an ITSM platform.

AI applications for ITSM help heads of I&O lower IT support costs, reduce service disruptions and improve the employee IT experience. These products automate repetitive IT support tasks and provide IT agents (both human and AI) with recommended actions for complex issues, such as change management or problem investigation. For example, a virtual support agent can resolve routine service requests without human intervention, while AI-driven analysis can detect patterns in incident data to identify connected service issues. These solutions also generate and summarize IT knowledge and communications, ensuring accurate and timely information is shared with users and stakeholders.

GenAI allows IT agents to create process automations and workflows using natural language prompts, ensuring consistent and efficient execution of routine tasks. Agentic ITSM capabilities provide nondeterministic automation for more complex or dynamic scenarios, allowing IT operations to respond intelligently and quickly to incidents.

By deploying AI applications for ITSM, heads of I&O can lower operational costs, improve resolution times, deliver higher service reliability, and provide a more responsive IT support experience.

AI Use-Case Assessment for IT Service Desk provides further details on the AI and GenAI opportunities that tools such as AI applications for ITSM are able to address.
